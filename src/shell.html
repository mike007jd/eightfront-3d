Base shooting and target markers

Regular base rooms need you to sidestep into the target column: standing, prone and jumping change the gun line, and the mouse cannot replace posture across columns. Release left/right and SHIFT first, then hold Down to fire prone; high cores need Down released and a jumping shot.

The small reticle previews where the centre ray lands, not every spread pellet. Solid targets can be hit; a closed marker means temporarily protected, and a shrinking armor bar still counts as a hit. Once every core is destroyed, any player moves forward into the next room.

Two players and gamepads

P2: arrow keys move, / fire, . jump, , switch weapon, ' grenade. Numpad 1 / 2 / 0 / 3 also work. No friendly fire; when one player is out the other continues.

Gamepad: D-pad or left stick to move, A jump, X / RT fire, Y switch weapon, RB grenade, Start pause. In menus the D-pad selects, A confirms, B goes back.

Advanced moves and retro mode

↓ + Jump: drop through one-way platforms. Modern mode lets you hold to fire and tap for a short hop, and P1 can aim with the mouse; regular base rooms still depend on position and posture. In retro mode R / S / F fire on tap and M / L on hold, with fixed jump height and eight-way aim. It is not a full NES simulation.

SETTINGS

Display & assist

Does not change difficulty, damage or aim. With guidance off, hits, protection state and exit direction still show.

Advanced & diagnostics

F3: frame stats. F8: hide UI. Measure performance on the target device.
